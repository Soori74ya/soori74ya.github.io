    황수현의 신작 《음 ⎯⎯》은 정관헌(靜觀軒)이라는 공간이 함의하고 있는 ‘조용히 내다본다’는 행위를 조명한다. 바라본다는 시각적 감각과 저 너머를 내다본다는 예기적 감각 사이에서 안무가는 ‘본다’는 감각 행위의 의미를 반추한다.

  description: |-
    Hwang Soo Hyun’s new work 《Hmmmm》 brings into focus the act of “quietly looking out,” implied by the space of Jeonggwanheon (靜觀軒). Between the visual perception of looking and the anticipatory perception of looking beyond, the choreographer reflects on the meaning of seeing as a sensory act.

    《Hmmmm》 invites the audience to look out at the landscape and remain in a state of suspension that defers interpretation. In this state, the familiar process of gathering to watch something and search for meaning is temporarily brought to a halt, creating an indeterminate and ambiguous void. Within this vacuum, the audience unexpectedly begins to sense a world beyond meaning-making.
