  title: I Want to Cry but I'm Not Sad
  workType: Live Performance
  startDate: 2016-09-24
  endDate: 2016-10-01
  venue: National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea
  production: Korea National Contemporary Dance Company, National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art
  credit: |-
    Concept/Choreography: Hwang Soohyun
    Performers: Kang Hojung, Hwang Dasom, Choi Seungyoon, Choi Minseon, Ma Kwanghyun, Choe Youngtak, Hyun Ziyea, Lim Jungha
    Music: Nam Sangwon
    Costume Design: Chungpepe
    Documentation: Lee Jinmyung

    Co-organized by: National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea; Korea National Contemporary Dance Company
  description: Emotion is used as a means of connecting body and mind. An individual’s embodied emotional information is activated by external stimuli, which in turn generate internal stimuli and manifest through the same performativity. By structuring emotions commonly understood as belonging to the private realm, this work explores how an individual’s emotions relate to other members of a group and the surrounding environment.
