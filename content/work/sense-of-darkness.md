en:
  title: Sense of Darkness
  workType: Live Performance
  startDate: 2019-10-10
  venue: Daehakro Arts Theater Small Theater
  production: Seoul Performing Arts Festival 2019
  description: |-
    This work experiments with whether bodily sensations stored through personal experience can be shared through another person’s body. With their eyes closed, the performers use sound to sense the space and their positions within it. Their movements and sounds stimulate the audience’s bodily experience, exploring how another person’s sensory experience can connect with one’s own.
