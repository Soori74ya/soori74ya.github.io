  description: 《Sync de Sync》 focuses on a sensory state of inquiry that emerges in the subtle gap between attunement and rupture. Movement, sound, and speech do not explain one another; each exists with its own sensory density, remaining close to and flowing with the body. The work slightly shifts the texture of familiar sensations, leaves discrepancies unresolved, and allows states of non-attunement to remain. In doing so, it forms a “physical network of relations” generated by unaligned sensations and explores, through practice, how we might move alongside the invisible.
