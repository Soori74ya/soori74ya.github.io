  description: Stored Reality begins with questions about the viewing of dance performances and experiments with the immediacy and ephemerality of the form. This work consists of three performances and one video. Audience members move between studios to view each of the performances in a random sequence, and on their way home, they receive a video composed of images from the performance on their mobile phones. What does it mean to watch a performance? How do sensations and imagination meet within a single experience? Within the cyclic loop of play and restoration of images, the boundaries of the performance become ambiguous.
