en:
  title: Co-Lab : Seoul-Berlin
  workType: Collaborative Work
  startDate: 2012-01-19
  venue: Arko Arts Theater Small Theater
  production: Hwang Soohyun
  description: |-
    Co-Lab : Seoul-Berlin brings to the stage the process of collaborative creation between two choreographers communicating online across the physical distance between Seoul and Berlin. Conversations, misunderstandings, empathy, and clashes arising across different times and spaces are transformed into movement, video, objects, and text. Online communication is revisited through the physical presence of bodies meeting on stage. The project leaves room for unfinished dialogue and the evolving process of communication, rather than a definitive outcome.
